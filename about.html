Our Story

Born From a Vision of Architectural Refinement

AURA ESTATES was founded in 2012 by a group of seasoned real estate professionals and NRI investors who recognised a critical gap in Tamil Nadu's luxury property market — the absence of a truly confidential, white-glove brokerage service for ultra-high-net-worth principals.

From our flagship advisory office in Poes Garden, Chennai, we have quietly guided industrialists, film personalities, NRI returnees, and business dynasties through some of Tamil Nadu's most significant property acquisitions — many never advertised publicly.

Today, AURA ESTATES operates across Chennai's most prestigious enclaves including ECR Oceanfront, Poes Garden, and Boat Club; as well as the Nilgiris, Coimbatore Race Course Road, and Mahabalipuram coastal belt — offering clients an unmatched portfolio of architectural masterworks.

What Drives Us

Our Core Principles

Every client engagement is governed by these immovable values — the bedrock of AURA ESTATES' reputation.

Absolute Confidentiality

Every transaction, inquiry, and client detail is handled under the strictest NDA-governed protocols. Discretion is not a service feature — it is our foundation.

Curated Excellence

We represent only the top 1% of Tamil Nadu's architectural estate market — properties that meet our rigorous standards of design, provenance, and location.

Fiduciary Commitment

As your exclusive advisor, our loyalty is entirely to you. We structure negotiations, due diligence, and legal processes to serve your financial interests above all.

NRI Specialist Access

Dedicated NRI desks in Singapore, Dubai, and the UK coordinate seamless property ownership, FEMA compliance, and remote due diligence for global clients.

Data-Driven Advisory

Our proprietary Tamil Nadu micro-market analytics model tracks 12+ enclave-level data points to guide acquisition timing, pricing, and ROI optimisation.

RERA Certified Practice

All advisors are RERA-certified, and every listing is legally vetted. We maintain zero-tolerance for encumbered properties, ensuring clean, court-clear titles.

Our Journey

A Decade of Milestones

Key chapters in the AURA ESTATES story, from inception to Tamil Nadu's most trusted luxury advisory.

2012

Foundation in Poes Garden

AURA ESTATES launches its flagship office in Chennai's most coveted enclave, with an inaugural portfolio of 6 off-market bungalows.

2015

ECR Oceanfront Division Opens

Expansion to the East Coast Road luxury segment — securing beachfront palaces and private villa communities for HNI clientele.

2017

Nilgiris & Coimbatore Presence

Launch of the Hill Estates division, curating heritage bungalows, tea-estate villas, and Race Course Road penthouses for discerning buyers.

2019

NRI Global Advisory Desks

Opened representative desks in Dubai, Singapore, and London to serve Tamil diaspora investors and returning NRI professionals.

2021

₹1,000 Crore Milestone

AURA ESTATES crossed ₹1,000 Crore in total transaction value — a landmark in Tamil Nadu's luxury real estate brokerage history.

2024

Digital Private Desk Launch

Introduction of our immersive 360° virtual tour platform and AI-powered property matching for global clients — redefining the luxury search experience.
